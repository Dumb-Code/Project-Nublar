## KQ1. Egg blocks: storage, ticking, crack stages, hatch, imprint, trample, break, render

### Takeaway
A dinosaur egg is a plain Block with no block entity. The species is the block type itself (one block per species, bound to an entity type through a holder), the only per-egg state is three blockstate properties (crack stage 0-2, needs-player flag, egg count 1-4), and hatching is a random-tick process: each random tick the egg has a 1 in 20 chance (1 in 10 on Fern Thatch) to advance, and the third success spawns the babies. At default random-tick speed this gives a mean of about 82,000 ticks (3.4 game days; 1.7 on Fern Thatch), not the wiki's 8 to 10 days. Trampling uses a 25-block cube (not 32), and imprinting uses the nearest player within 10 blocks, but only for three of the seven species.

### Inferences
- [inf] A datapack-driven species list cannot use "one block per species". Nublar needs per-egg data (species id, hatch deadline or progress, clutch size, optional quality) and therefore a block entity, or one block with a data component on the item plus a block entity on the placed block. Alex's Caves is evidence only for the visual stage model (3 textures) and the tick-free cost profile.
- [inf] Alex's Caves hatch time is random with a coefficient of variation near 58% and depends on chunk simulation. A block entity that stores a hatch game-time and the three stage times, driven by scheduled ticks (or one sparse ticker), keeps the same near-zero cost and gives a deterministic, datapack-tunable time. This is a design inference, not an Alex's Caves feature.
- [inf] The "2 times faster on Fern Thatch" rule is the only environmental modifier. It maps cleanly to a nest-bedding block tag with a speed factor in datapack data.
- [inf] The 25-cube provocation and the "no provocation on mining" rule are cheap to reproduce (one entity query per crush event) and need no per-tick state.

## KQ2. Vallumraptor: elder, packs, hunting, flee, chest raiding, calming

### Takeaway
A Vallumraptor pack is a transient singly-linked chain (each member stores one prior and one after reference; nothing is saved) headed by an Elder, with a maximum of 8 members. Followers do not actively follow: they only bias their random wandering toward the head, and the Elder copies its own attack target onto every member of the chain every tick. Elders exist only because the first animal of a naturally spawned group has a 50% chance to be one. When the head dies, the chain dissolves within a few ticks; "flee when the Elder dies" in the wiki is really "flee from a Grottoceratops when the pack is smaller than 4". Chest raiding is a block search every 100 to 200 ticks plus a one-item theft.

### Cited Findings
- Elder choice and marking.
  - Effect: some raptors look bigger (scale 1.1) with a different texture, have more health and armour, and lead packs.
  - Mechanism: the Elder flag is a synced boolean, saved in NBT. It is set only in `finalizeSpawn`: if the spawn call arrives with group data that is a vanilla animal group-data object and the group size is zero the animal is the Elder; if no group data arrives (the first animal of a group) a fair coin decides. In vanilla's group logic the group size is raised after the first animal, so the "size zero" branch is not reached by later members: in practice only the first animal of a natural group can be an Elder, with 50% chance. Hatched and bred raptors never go through `finalizeSpawn`, so they are never Elders. Nothing else sets or clears the flag. [v: entity/living/VallumraptorEntity.java L411-417] [v: entity/living/VallumraptorEntity.java L459-469] [inf on the vanilla group-data counter] The renderer enlarges Elders by 10% and switches to an Elder texture. [v: main:client/render/entity/VallumraptorRenderer.java L37-53]
  - Stats: base attributes are 28 health, speed 0.2, attack 3, follow range 32. When the Elder flag first reads true the tick sets base health 32, armour 5 and heals by 36; if the flag later reads false it sets health 24 and armour 0. So a non-Elder spawn has 28 health, while the wiki says 24 (24 is only the value after a demotion that cannot happen in normal play). ## KQ3. Tremorsaurus: taming, commands, roar fear, aggression, provocation

## KQ5. Babies, growth, breeding cooldown, clutches

### Takeaway
A baby is a vanilla baby: hatching sets the age to -24000 and the game's normal ageing turns it into an adult in 24000 ticks (one game day, 20 real minutes) with no stages. Breeding uses vanilla's love state and a 6000-tick (5 minute) cooldown, then a bred mother carries an egg flag, walks to bare ground, buries an egg for 100 ticks, and places an egg block (1 to 3 eggs for raptors and Subterranodons, 1 otherwise) and a Fern Thatch bed. The numbers in the wiki (1 day, 5 minutes, clutch 1 to 4) match the code, except that 4 is the stack limit of the block, not a laying outcome.

## KQ8. Mapping to Project Nublar modules, and conflicts with vanilla feel

### Takeaway
Alex's Caves supplies exact numbers and workable mechanisms for four things Nublar needs: a stage-based egg with a Fern Thatch style bedding bonus (B11), a leader-flag pack with shared target and dissolve-on-leader-loss (B18), a stun-then-calm taming ritual with a counter plus a nap-then-calm path for scavengers plus nearest-player imprint (B19), and a roar-as-threat-display that frightens nearby mobs (B19). It does not supply brooding guards, smooth growth, hunger-driven feeding, per-species datapack data, or low-cost scans: those are gaps Nublar must fill itself.

### Cited Findings
- B11 synthesis, egg printer, incubator and hatch.
  - Reusable: three crack stages with three textures; a hatch routine (remove block, spawn one baby per egg, set baby age, imprint on the nearest player within a radius, sounds, particles); nest bedding that speeds hatching; an optional "needs a player within N blocks" flag on world-generated eggs; Silk-Touch-only pickup; dinosaurs ignore egg collisions. [v: block/DinosaurEggBlock.java L108-145] [v: block/DinosaurEggBlock.java L104-106]
  - Not reusable: species-per-block (fixed seven species), random-tick hatching (high variance), no per-egg data. The default decision D05 (block entity) is supported by this contrast.
- B16 dinosaur core.
  - Reusable: baby as negative age with a species scale; list of baby differences (smaller scale, no roar scare, bite only, cannot fly, cannot be ridden, browses low, no player targeting for raptors); leader stat bonus (Elder: more health and armour); passive regeneration 2 health per 100 ticks for every species; dimension refresh at baby change. [v: entity/living/DinosaurEntity.java L84-111]
  - Not reusable: seven-part sauropod bodies with per-tick part repositioning (Nublar has its own hitbox design). [v: entity/living/SauropodBaseEntity.java L247-305]
- B17 needs.
  - Reusable: food lists per species (see KQ5); tempt items; "eating a plant heals 5"; periodic grazing with a visible chew animation; scavenging one item at a time from chests; browse heights split by age.
  - Not reusable as is: grazing and chest raiding are timer-driven, not need-driven; ring scans are expensive (KQ6).
- B18 social life and breeding.
  - Reusable: Elder flag at group spawn; join rule (nearest chain tail whose head is a valid leader, size cap, distance cap 26 for leaving); loose cohesion by biasing wander toward the leader; leader-to-members target push; pack-size morale flee; flock formation flight; breeding loop (love, walk together, has-egg flag, walk to bare ground, bury 100 ticks, place clutch and bedding); 5-minute cooldown; clutch of 1 to 3. [v: entity/ai/AnimalJoinPackGoal.java L26-82] [v: entity/ai/AnimalBreedEggsGoal.java L26-70] [v: entity/ai/AnimalLayEggGoal.java L34-86]
  - Missing: guard behaviour for eggs and young (only trample revenge), herd leader for Grottoceratops, explicit leader replacement when the Elder dies.
- B19 player interaction and detection.
  - Reusable: per-target-class aggro (dinosaur prey 32, players 8 or 12); roar before attack as threat display (a 55-tick animation that blocks movement and biting; cooldown 200 to 399 ticks; idle chance 1 in 600 per tick); roar fear as a box effect (30 by 10 by 30, drop target, tremble, flee at double speed, weakness from a tame roarer); egg-trample provocation of the species within 25; taming by a stun item plus a calm item with a persisted counter (first success at feed 4, certain at 9); taming by a repeated chance item (1 in 3); calming a scavenger during a nap; imprint at hatch; three-state command cycle by interaction (wander, stay, follow) with sneak required for rideable species; owner-hurt defence goals.   - Conflicts with vanilla feel: the stun and salad ritual, the roar scare, the hiding invisible raptor, and chest raiding are not vanilla; all are readable and tied to a visible cue (stun stars, roar animation, flee). - B21 worldgen. The Subterranodon roost (Fern Thatch pad, eggs with NEEDS_PLAYER, home-bound adult) is a small nest precedent. [v: level/feature/SubterranodonRoostFeature.java L74-96]

The patterns to avoid are the 61-block join scan without a negative cache, the per-tick roar box scan on both sides, the ring scans of 10^4 to 10^5 block reads per animal every few seconds, per-tick synced counters, and a global per-entity tick hook.

### Inferences
- [inf] Simplest faithful design per behaviour (no speculative abstraction):
  1. Egg: block entity (species id, start time, clutch size, bedding factor). Three scheduled ticks (crack 1, crack 2, hatch) computed from species data and the bedding factor; on hatch run the hatch routine of KQ1 with a datapack imprint radius.
  2. Pack: leader flag on the animal, a leader reference on followers; join check on a slow timer only for animals with no leader, with a result cached for a few seconds; leader target changes are pushed to followers once; leader loss makes every follower flee for a timer.
  3. Roar: on roar start, apply a "frightened until tick T" state to mobs in the box once; mobs read it in their flee goal. Tame roarer adds weakness once.
  4. Taming: per-species datapack fields (stun hit chance, stun ticks, first success feed, guaranteed feed, success chance); a saved counter on the animal.
  5. Provocation: on a crush event, query once, set targets with a timer.
  6. Scans: replace ring scans by tag-indexed or POI-indexed lookups (chests, plants, trees) refreshed on block change.
- [inf] Keep ranges as three numbers per species (horizontal, vertical, interval) to avoid the wiki's radius confusion.

### Gaps
- Nublar's own design documents were not re-read for this note; the mapping uses the module list in the assignment only.
- Alex's Caves has no egg incubator machine, no synthesis chain and no fence or vehicle interaction; nothing here maps to B11 machines beyond the egg block.
