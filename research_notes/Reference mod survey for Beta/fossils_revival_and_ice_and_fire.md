# Fossils and Archeology Revival and Ice and Fire CE: creature-raising loop and performance

Scope: gameplay systems and performance of the two closest open-source analogues to Project Nublar. Hitboxes are covered in `research_notes/Multipart entity hitbox systems/` and are only touched where growth scaling depends on them.

Label key: `[v]` verified in source at the stated commit, `[inf]` inference from the source, `[doc]` from README, wiki or docs. Every `[v]` claim links the file at the commit with line numbers. Mechanisms are described in words and numbered steps; no code is reproduced.

Link bases used below (GitHub accepts the 7-character commit prefix):

- FAR = `https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/` followed by a path under `common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/` unless the path starts with `forge/`, `fabric/` or `common/src/`.

Nublar module codes (B00 to B27) are the Beta modules in `docs/project-plan.md`. Performance ratings: **cheap** (constant work, or interval-gated), **moderate** (bounded scans or per-tick work on one entity), **expensive** (unbounded or large scans; would not meet 20 TPS with 100 dinosaurs without change).

## Q1. FAR machines: analyzer, culture vat, sifter, worktable, feeder

### Takeaway
All four processing machines share one furnace-like base: an integer progress counter that rises by 1 per tick while a recipe is valid and falls by 2 per tick when it is not, an optional energy drain (off by default), block-state `ACTIVE` flips only on state change, and vanilla `WorldlyContainer` sided slots for hoppers. The feeder is different: it converts items into per-food-type point pools every 5 ticks and dinosaurs drain the pool one point per tick while standing at it. Breaking any machine drops its contents through vanilla `Containers.dropContents`.

### Cited Findings

**Shared machine base**
- Effect: machines look and behave like furnaces (lit time, progress bar, hopper in from top or sides, out from bottom). Mechanism: an abstract `BaseContainerBlockEntity` holds `litTime`, `litDuration`, `cookingProgress`, `cookingTotalTime`, saved as shorts; subclasses implement `canProcess`, `createItem`, `getItems`. `[v]` [MachineContainerBlockEntity.java L17-L53](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/block/entity/MachineContainerBlockEntity.java#L17-L53). Energy-using machines add a `CommonEnergyStorage` created per platform (Forge Energy capability or Team Reborn Energy on Fabric) and persist it under `Energy`. `[v]` [EnergyContainerBlockEntity.java L9-L32](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/block/entity/EnergyContainerBlockEntity.java#L9-L32).
- Energy is optional: `machinesRequireEnergy` defaults false; when true, usage is 1 FE per tick, max stored 1000, transfer 10 per tick. `[v]` [ForgeConfig.java L99-L105](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/forge/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/config/forge/ForgeConfig.java#L99-L105).
- Item retention on break: `CustomEntityBlock.onRemove` drops the inventory when the block changes to a different block; `FeederBlock` does the same. `[v]` [CustomEntityBlock.java L48-L54](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/block/custom_blocks/CustomEntityBlock.java#L48-L54), [FeederBlock.java L41-L50](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/block/custom_blocks/FeederBlock.java#L41-L50).
- Perf: **cheap**. One counter per tick; `setChanged` and block updates only on completion or on the active-flag edge. Nublar: B12 (machine framework), B14 (power). Vanilla feel: none; this is the furnace idiom.

**Analyzer (fossil to DNA)**
- Effect: put up to 9 analyzable items in, get weighted random outputs in 4 output slots every 10 seconds. Mechanism, per server tick: (1) if energy is required and empty, decay progress by 2 and return; (2) scan input slots 0 to 8 for the first item with an analyzer recipe and check any of output slots 9 to 12 is empty; (3) if so, progress += 1 and, when it reaches 200 ticks, roll one output and insert it (merge into a same-item stack below 64 first, else first empty slot), shrink the input by 1; (4) else decay progress by 2; (5) flip `ACTIVE` only when the processing flag changes. `[v]` [AnalyzerBlockEntity.java L70-L162](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/block/entity/AnalyzerBlockEntity.java#L70-L162); duration constant 200. `[v]` [AnalyzerMenu.java L13](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/inventory/AnalyzerMenu.java#L13). Hopper faces: top inserts to 0 to 8, bottom extracts 9 to 12. `[v]` L194-L206 of the same file. If a recipe output has count greater than 1 the count is randomised 1 to count-1. `[v]` L140-L142.
- Output selection: recipes carry a `NavigableMap<Double, ItemStack>` of cumulative weights; the machine picks `higherEntry(random * lastKey)`. `[v]` [MultiOutputAndSlotsRecipe.java L27-L74](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/recipe/MultiOutputAndSlotsRecipe.java#L27-L74). A "nothing" output is a weighted entry too (for example chicken egg: 25 chicken DNA, 75 nothing). `[v]` [ModRecipeProvider.java L379](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/forge/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/forge/data/providers/ModRecipeProvider.java#L379).
- DNA odds (datagen, weights out of 100): bio fossil gives sand 35, bone meal 50, and the remaining 15 split evenly over all Mesozoic species DNA; shale fossil the same with tuff for Paleozoic species; tar fossil gives bone meal 15, volcanic rock 30, and 25 split over Cenozoic species plus ten vanilla mobs; each individual bone item gives bone meal 30, bone 35, that species' DNA 35; frozen meat gives four vanilla meats at 15 each and tar fossil 20 plus the tar DNA pool; Failuresaurus flesh gives rotten flesh 33 and 67 split over every species. `[v]` [ModRecipeProvider.java L265-L372](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/forge/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/forge/data/providers/ModRecipeProvider.java#L265-L372). Analyzing a species' meat, egg or embryo gives that DNA at 100. `[v]` L343-L357.
- Recipe lookup is memoised per input item in a static map, so the per-tick recipe check is a hash lookup after the first call. `[v]` [ModRecipes.java L135-L139](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/recipe/ModRecipes.java#L135-L139).
- Perf: **cheap**, but note the memo map is never invalidated on `/reload` and is keyed on `Item` only (ignores NBT). `[inf]` Nublar: B05 (processor), B13 (recipes). Vanilla feel: none.

**Culture vat (DNA to egg or embryo)**
- Effect: DNA plus Bio-Goo fuel becomes an egg item, embryo item or plant after 5 minutes, with a 10% chance the vat "breaks out" and spawns a failure mob. Mechanism per tick: (1) energy gate as above; (2) decrement lit time; (3) if a recipe matches and the vat is not lit (or the lit fuel no longer matches), consume one fuel and set lit time to the recipe's `fuelDuration` (default 6000) and remember the fuel item; (4) while lit and valid, progress += 1; at 6000 ticks create the output (place or grow the output stack) and shrink input; (5) decay by 2 when unlit or invalid; (6) flip `ACTIVE` on lit edge and the `EMBRYO` block property (NONE, TREE, PLANT, LIMBLESS, INSECT, GENERIC, chosen by item tags) on processing edge; (7) at exactly progress 3001, roll `cultureVatFailChance` (default 10) percent to call the failure path. `[v]` [CultureVatBlockEntity.java L101-L180](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/block/entity/CultureVatBlockEntity.java#L101-L180); constants 6000 (100 in debug builds). `[v]` [CultureVatMenu.java L20-L24](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/inventory/CultureVatMenu.java#L20-L24). Changing the input item resets progress to 0. `[v]` L245-L257.
- Failure: messages every player within a 100-block cube, then replaces the vat with a mutant plant or mutant sapling for plant inputs, or spawns a mob by a 0 to 99 roll (under 5 creeper, under 10 piglin, under 15 zombie horse, further bands follow) for animal inputs. `[v]` [CultureVatBlock.java L60-L85](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/block/custom_blocks/CultureVatBlock.java#L60-L85). The wiki describes the same: 10% default, destroys the vat, Failuresaurus or rarely creeper, zombie horse, mooshroom, zombified piglin. `[doc]` [Culture Vat wiki](https://fossilsarcheology.wiki.gg/wiki/Culture_Vat).
- Recipe JSON shape: `type fossil:culture_vat`, `duration 6000`, `fuel {item fossil:bio_goo}`, `input {item <dna>}`, `result <egg item>`; one file per species. `[v]` [generated culture_vat/alligator_gar_dna.json](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/generated/resources/data/fossil/recipes/culture_vat/alligator_gar_dna.json).
- Perf: **cheap**; recipe lookup is a vanilla `RecipeManager.getRecipeFor` per tick while a recipe is being checked (not memoised like the analyzer). `[v]` [CultureVatBlockEntity.java L182-L197](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/block/entity/CultureVatBlockEntity.java#L182-L197). Nublar: B11 (synthesis and egg printer), B13. Vanilla feel: the failure spawn of hostile mobs is a deliberate risk mechanic; Nublar has no equivalent.

- Perf: block tick **cheap** (three slots, one conversion per 5 ticks). ### Inferences
- `[inf]` A single "progress counter that decays at 2x when invalid" base class serves four machines in FAR with under 300 lines each; Nublar's B12 framework can do the same and should treat per-recipe `duration` and `fuelDuration` as recipe JSON fields rather than machine constants, as FAR's changelog explicitly added them.
- `[inf]` FAR keeps food stored in the feeder as integer point pools rather than items, which makes eating a one-integer decrement per tick and lets a single feeder serve many animals without stack juggling. This maps well to Nublar's B20 "hunger actually increases on eating" fix.

### Gaps
- The hopper-support claim relies on `WorldlyContainer` sided slots; whether Fabric Transfer API pipes honour this was not verified.
- The analyzer recipe memo map's behaviour across `/reload` was not traced further.

## Q2. FAR eggs and incubation

### Takeaway
A FAR egg is a `LivingEntity` (not a block) with 2 health. It hatches after 3000 ticks of warmth (2.5 minutes), counts down instead when too cold or wet, and on hatch spawns a day-0 baby that imprints on the nearest player within 16 blocks if the species' taming type is IMPRINTING. Golden eggs hatch 10% sooner.

## Q3. FAR growth stages and scaling

### Takeaway
Age is a tick counter that increments every server tick. Scale, health, damage, armour, knockback resistance and speed are linear interpolations between a base and max value over `adultAgeDays` x 24000 ticks; "baby" and "teen" are only thresholds on days for behaviour and textures, not for size steps. The age is synced to clients only every 120 ticks, and the hitbox is rebuilt from the entity type's dimensions times the scale with a free-position search so a growing animal does not clip into blocks.

### Cited Findings
- Species data fields: `adultAgeDays`, `teenAgeDays`, `scaleBase`, `scaleMax`, `eggScale`, `maxHunger`, `maxPopulation` (default 15), `canBeRidden`, `breaksBlocks`, `diet`, attribute ranges (`healthBase/Max`, `damageBase/Max`, `speedBase/Min/Max`, `sprintMod` default 1.4, swim speeds, `armorBase/Max`, `knockBackResistanceBase/Max`) and the `ai` block (activity, attacking, climbing, moving, response, taming). `[v]` [EntityDataLoader.java L58-L98](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/entity/data/EntityDataLoader.java#L58-L98), [Attribute.java L12-L53](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/entity/data/Attribute.java#L12-L53). Example Tyrannosaurus: adult 12 days, teen 5, health 15 to 60, damage 2 to 14, speed 0.15 to 0.25, armour 0 to 7, knockback resistance 0 to 1, max hunger 150, max population 2, egg scale 1.25, scale 0.2 to 2.6, diet CARNIVORE, ridable, breaks blocks, DIURNAL, DROP attack, AGGRESSIVE, GEM taming. `[v]` [tyrannosaurus.json](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/resources/data/fossil/entity_info/tyrannosaurus.json).

Movement speed attribute to blocks per second is approximated by a fitted quadratic. `[v]` [Util.java L94-L97](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/entity/util/Util.java#L94-L97).
- Stage thresholds: baby = days < teenAgeDays; teen = teenAgeDays ≤ days < adultAgeDays; adult = days ≥ adultAgeDays. `[v]` L953-L968. Babies and fleeing animals report response SCARED regardless of species. `[v]` L1563-L1568. Babies cannot hunt. `[v]` [HuntingTargetGoal.java L53](https://github.com/TeamFossilsArcheology/FossilsArcheologyRevival/blob/b400cf2/common/src/main/java/com/github/teamfossilsarcheology/fossil/entity/ai/HuntingTargetGoal.java#L53). Only adults may be ridden by whip and only adults break blocks. `[v]` L1290, L884.
- Hitbox refresh on growth: compute new dimensions; if larger, build a voxel shape of the size difference and call `level.findFreePosition`; if none is free, keep the old dimensions and sync them to the client via a `DIMENSION_VER` compound tag. ### Inferences
- `[inf]` FAR's 120-tick sync interval is the key trick for 100 animals: the client receives a scale update 0.5 times per second per animal instead of 20, and the server rebuilds the AABB at the same rate. Nublar's B16 should sync maturity at an interval and refresh `EntityDimensions` only then.
- `[inf]` Because `scaleBase` for a T. rex is 0.2 and `scaleMax` 2.6, a hatchling is 13x smaller than an adult in linear size; FAR relies on `findFreePosition` on every growth step to keep large animals out of walls. Nublar's B26 arrival logic could instead pause growth while the body is in contact with blocks, which is cheaper.

## Q4. FAR hunger, food values, mood, taming and orders

### Takeaway
Hunger is an integer up to `maxHunger` that drops by 1 every 60 seconds; below 75% the animal is "hungry" and seeks food, below 25% "deadly hungry" and sprints to food, at 0 it takes 1 damage every 2 seconds (to death only if `enableStarvation`). Food values come from a datapack per food type with vanilla-nutrition multipliers. Mood is a -100..100 integer driven mainly by two periodic environment checks (plants nearby, population) plus event deltas; it gates breeding, playing, aggression towards players, and owner loyalty. Taming has four species-defined routes, and tamed animals cycle three orders (stay, follow, wander) with a per-species order item.

## Q5. FAR herding and flocking

### Takeaway
Flocking is opt-in per species through a `PrehistoricFlocking` subclass: a leader is any animal whose follower count exceeds 1, followers path to within about 12 blocks of the leader every 25 ticks, groups build by age preference (adults lead), and a follower leaves if the leader dies or is not found within 32 blocks on a 1-in-200 per-tick check. Herd membership changes temperament: a Gallimimus in a group of 3 or more becomes TERRITORIAL instead of SCARED.

### Cited Findings
- State: `groupSize` (starts 1), `groupLeader` reference, `flockAttackedTick` and `flockAttackedTarget`. ## Q6. FAR mounts and riding

### Takeaway

## Q8. FAR player detection and attack

### Takeaway

## Q11. IaF CE dragon growth stages and scaling

### Takeaway
A dragon ages one tick per server tick with no cap on the counter; stage is a step function of days (1 under 25, 2 at 25, 3 at 50, 4 at 75, 5 at 100), while render size interpolates linearly inside each stage between a per-stage min and max (1 to 3, 3 to 7, 7 to 12.5, 12.5 to 20, 20 to 30) and freezes after day 125. Attributes are linear in days over 0 to 125 with per-type min and max (health 20 to 500, damage 1 to 18, speed 0.15 to 0.4, armour 1 to 20 by default) and are refreshed once per in-game day. ## Q15. IaF CE: keeping dragon ticks cheap (intervals, lazy pathing, flight navigation)

## Q17. Cross-cutting numbers and vanilla compatibility

### Takeaway
The two mods bracket Nublar's design: FAR runs smooth growth over 5 to 12 in-game days with hunger in the hundreds decaying per minute; IaF runs staged growth over 100+ days with hunger 0 to 100 decaying every 2.5 minutes. Both keep vanilla intact by adding goals to vanilla mobs on join, using tags for diets and targets, registering creatures as CREATURE with forced persistence, and never replacing vanilla entities.

### Inferences
- `[inf]` Nublar's planned numbers sit between the two: growth longer than FAR (45 days vs 12) but far shorter than IaF (100+), hunger decay between FAR's 60 s and IaF's 150 s. FAR's "hungry at 75%, urgent at 25%" two-threshold model is a direct fit for Nublar's "need cooldown" and "chase ends at zero stamina" design (seek food early, sprint when urgent).
- `[inf]` Both mods confirm that `MobCategory.CREATURE` plus forced persistence works for never-spawning, never-despawning park animals; Nublar's B16 decision to prefer CREATURE over MONSTER is consistent with both.
- `[inf]` Neither mod implements a warning display, sound or scent detection, stamina, thirst, or a live-bait mechanic; those Nublar modules (B17 thirst and stamina, B19 detection channels and threat display, B20 live bait) have no reference implementation in either repository and must be designed from Nublar's own documents.
