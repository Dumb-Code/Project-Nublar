## Q1. Which mods use multipart parts, of what design, how many, how positioned?

### Takeaway
Every boss mod with public source that uses parts builds them on unspawned NeoForge/Forge `PartEntity` subclasses (Twilight Forest, Cataclysm, Untamed Wilds, Iron's Spells, MHLib), placed procedurally on the server from the parent yaw/pitch with hard-coded offsets; only MHLib places parts from GeckoLib bones, and it does so by trusting one client. Two designs avoid `PartEntity`: BOMD (one entity whose `getBoundingBox()` returns an AABB subclass that carries oriented boxes) and Aquamirae (its own loader-neutral part system built from mixins, the only one that runs on Fabric and NeoForge from common code).

### Cited Findings

**Twilight Forest (26.1.2 NeoForge; the most current code in this survey)**

This shows `PartEntity` still exists in NeoForge for MC 26.1.2.
- Part state (x, y, z, yRot, xRot, width, height, fixed flag, dirty synched data) is server-authoritative and sent with `UpdateTFMultipartPacket`; the send is hooked into `ServerEntity.sendDirtyEntityData` by an ASM transformer — [TFPart.writeData/readData](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/TFPart.java), [SendDirtyEntityDataTransformer](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/asm/java/twilightforest/asm/transformers/multipart/SendDirtyEntityDataTransformer.java), [MultipartHooks](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/asmhooks/MultipartHooks.java).
- Naga: main entity 2.0 x 3.0 ([TFEntities L81](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/init/TFEntities.java#L81)); up to `MAX_SEGMENTS = 12` `NagaSegment` parts of 2 x 2; the number of active segments follows health (2 to 12), and removed segments are shrunk to 0 x 0 and made invisible rather than removed — [Naga.java L70-L77, L185-L205](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/boss/Naga.java#L185-L205), [NagaSegment.activate/deactivate](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/boss/NagaSegment.java#L111-L119).

- Hitbox layouts ("profiles") load from datapacks; parts are `MHLibPartEntity` (a `PartEntity`) created per `SubPartConfig` — [IMultipartEntity.createSubPartsFromProfile](https://github.com/ZigyTheBird/MultiHitBoxLib/blob/9b06d5cfa6577057d60e73a9aaa090b51237626e/src/main/java/de/dertoaster/multihitboxlib/api/IMultipartEntity.java); feature list on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/multihitboxlib).

### Inferences
- Part sync splits into three camps: (a) server-authoritative positions plus a custom packet (TF, Cataclysm), (b) vanilla-style deterministic placement computed on both sides (Untamed Wilds, Iron's Spells, Hydra/Leviathan offsets), (c) client-reported bones (MHLib). Camp (c) gives bone accuracy at the cost of trusting one client and of 1+ tick lag; camps (a) and (b) are what the candidate hybrid already implies (per-species spine computed on the server).
- Aquamirae proves that the NeoForge `PartEntity` behaviour can be re-built with about four common-code mixins, so a Fabric port of section hit parts does not need a third-party library. Its mixin targets (`Level.getEntities`, `getEntityOrPart`, tracking callbacks) are the surface to re-check on 26.2.
- Iron's Spells' "offsets in model pixels x `getScale()`" is the closest existing match to Project Nublar's smooth growth: the part table can be authored once against the adult model and scaled by growth.

### Gaps
- Could not confirm whether Aquamirae's mixins compile unchanged on 26.x (the read branch targets `[1.21.1, 1.22)`).
- MHLib's original repository (DerToaster98) was not located; a copy at an older version (1.2.0) was read, while the latest is listed as 1.8.1.
- Cataclysm's `MessageCMMultipart` was not read in detail; the sync cadence is assumed to mirror TF.

## Q2. Does any mod let parts or body shape affect collision, pathfinding or turning? How, and does it work?

### Takeaway
No surveyed mod lets parts drive block collision or pathfinding. Parts only push or damage entities; terrain collision and pathing always use the single vanilla main box. Body size enters navigation only through width-aware waypoint tolerances and node re-centring, plus block breaking for bosses. Turning is handled by yaw-rate limits and "turn until aligned, then thrust" in attack goals, not by any body-shape check.

### Cited Findings
- TF Hydra and Naga segments push and hurt entities inside their boxes (`collideWithEntities`, `collideWithOthers` → `entity.push(this)`), but the Hydra itself reports `isPushable() == false`, ignores `doPush` and `knockback` — [Hydra L212-L213, L696-L706](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/boss/Hydra.java#L696-L706), [NagaSegment L85-L109](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/boss/NagaSegment.java#L85-L109).
- TF Hydra body/tail boxes destroy blocks they overlap (`destroyBlocksInAABB(body/tail box)`) — [Hydra L299-L300](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/boss/Hydra.java#L299-L300).
- Iron's Spells parts have an optional `collision` flag returned by `canBeCollidedWith()` (default false); a commented-out `canCollideWith` shows an attempt to let players stand on top — [IceSpiderPartEntity L43-L52](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/entity/mobs/ice_spider/IceSpiderPartEntity.java#L43-L52).
- MultipartEntities can turn the OBB union into a `VoxelShape` (voxelised at 1/4 block) for collision, but only when no override box is set; BOMD sets an override collision box on every layout (Gauntlet 2x4x2, fist 2x2x2) — [CompoundOrientedBox.toVoxelShape L115-L145](https://github.com/barribob/MultipartEntities/blob/895e77a82ccc695cfd13d68c9638ce3b5f6bb458/src/main/java/io/github/stuff_stuffs/multipart_entities/common/util/CompoundOrientedBox.java#L115-L145), [GauntletHitboxes L20-L43](https://github.com/barribob/bosses-of-mass-destruction/blob/2fbd0dc79bea498bcad755c4ad9969055dc452c7/src/main/kotlin/net/barribob/boss/mob/mobs/gauntlet/GauntletHitboxes.kt#L20-L43).
- MultipartEntities' `isInsideWall` uses the compound box shrunk by 0.1 — [AbstractMultipartAwareLivingEntity L74-L78](https://github.com/barribob/MultipartEntities/blob/895e77a82ccc695cfd13d68c9638ce3b5f6bb458/src/main/java/io/github/stuff_stuffs/multipart_entities/common/entity/AbstractMultipartAwareLivingEntity.java#L74-L78).

### Inferences
- The field consensus matches the candidate hybrid's first rule: keep terrain collision and pathing on one vanilla box and use parts only for hits and entity pushes. ## Q3. How do segmented long bodies (Naga, serpents, worms) move through terrain?

### Takeaway
Segments are never physically simulated against blocks. TF's Naga uses a follow-the-leader chain with a fixed 2-block link and a straightening bias, lifts any segment that ends up inside a wall, and lets the head clear leaves (and, when angered, any breakable block) around itself; Untamed Wilds' anaconda replays the head's past yaw from a ring buffer. Only the head's vanilla box pathfinds.

### Cited Findings
- Naga `moveSegments()`: segment i follows segment i-1 (or the head) at a fixed distance `f = 2.0`; the direction is the normalised vector from leader to segment plus a "straighten" term along the leader's back direction of strength `0.05 + 0.5/(i+1)` (front segments straighten more); if a segment `isInWall()`, its target Y is raised by 2; positions are set with `setPos` (no `move()`, so no block collision) — [Naga L466-L508](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/boss/Naga.java#L466-L508).
- Naga terrain handling: when mob griefing is allowed it destroys leaves (and, if `shouldDestroyAllBlocks()`, any destroyable block) in its box inflated by 0.75 horizontally; if it falls more than 5 blocks below its home point it teleports home — [Naga L258-L293](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/boss/Naga.java#L258-L293).
- Naga path following: nodes within `bbWidth * 4` (8 blocks) of the head are skipped, so the head steers toward a point well ahead — [Naga L295-L306](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/boss/Naga.java#L295-L306).
- Naga slither: `NagaMoveControl` drives strafe input `xxa = cos(tick * 0.3) * 0.6` except while charging, intimidating or dazed — [NagaMoveControl](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/ai/control/NagaMoveControl.java).
- Naga gains speed as segments are lost (`ADD_VALUE` modifier `MAX_SEGMENTS / segments * 0.02`) — [Naga L199-L203](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/boss/Naga.java#L199-L203).
- Untamed Wilds anaconda: each tick writes yaw and Y into a ring buffer; segments are placed from buffered yaw/Y differences (`getMovementOffsets(5) - getMovementOffsets(10)` etc.), with `xo/yo/zo` set to the previous position for interpolation; segments call `collideWithNearbyEntities()` — [EntityAnaconda L147-L200](https://github.com/RayTrace082/untamedwilds/blob/4891218ae8d56d15f1689cee17e5aca9e96ca99a/src/main/java/untamedwilds/entity/reptile/EntityAnaconda.java#L147-L200).
- TF #2352 (TF 4.6.3026): Naga renders half sunk into the ground — [issue](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/issues/2352).

### Inferences
- A chain (TF) keeps the body length constant and naturally follows the head's actual route, which is what a long-tailed dinosaur's tail should do; a ring buffer (Untamed Wilds, vanilla dragon) is cheaper but replays heading, not position, so the tail can swing through blocks on tight turns.
- Neither approach stops the head from entering a gap the body cannot follow; both accept tails clipping through terrain. For a quadruped or biped dinosaur whose legs carry the body, the TF chain is a good model for the tail and neck parts only, not for movement.
- The Naga's "skip nodes within 4 x width" look-ahead is a crude turn-radius control: it forces wide arcs. That is the opposite of what herds in forests need, so the candidate's turn-in-place is the better fit for tight spaces.

### Gaps
- No public mod was found that simulates segment-block collision (for example, a worm that must route its whole body through a tunnel).

## Q4. How do herd or pack mobs avoid jams and keep spacing? Any non-circular spacing or formation logic?

### Takeaway
All surveyed herd systems are leader- or centroid-based with circular (centre-to-centre) spacing at best. Jurassic Reborn (inherited from JurassiCraft 2) is the only one with explicit separation, a shared herd heading, and jam recovery (reverse the heading when over a quarter of members fail to path). Prehistoric Fauna and Untamed Wilds have no separation at all. No mod uses non-circular or body-length-aware spacing.

### Cited Findings
- Jurassic Reborn `Herd` (1.12.2, JurassiCraft-derived, LGPL-2.1): a herd object owns members, leader, centroid and a heading (`moveX`, `moveZ`). For each idle member it picks a target = heading x 8 + pull toward centroid + separation from every other member; separation radius = `width * 1.5 + 1.5`; navigation target is the surface height at that X/Z; it skips repath if the current path end is within 25 of the new target, and only moves if the target is more than 4 blocks away — [Herd.java L124-L190](https://github.com/Interactive-Modding/Jurassic-Reborn/blob/4a9a21b6ad7cea718534abd31913cccca2a6d98e/src/main/java/mod/reborn/server/entity/ai/Herd.java#L124-L190).
- 1.12.2 bug: separation compares a squared distance (`getDistanceSq`) with a linear radius; the code carries `/*TODO:make sure works */` — [Herd.java L144-L149](https://github.com/Interactive-Modding/Jurassic-Reborn/blob/4a9a21b6ad7cea718534abd31913cccca2a6d98e/src/main/java/mod/reborn/server/entity/ai/Herd.java#L144-L149). The 1.21.1 port fixes it (`sepSq = sep * sep`, guards against zero distance) but now divides the centroid pull by squared distance — [Herd.java (1.21.1) L126-L147](https://github.com/Interactive-Modding/Jurassic-Reborn/blob/a6bfa8fe72a6222be3e37cc158dfa6c9b98da5c8/src/main/java/net/vit/jurassicreborn/common/entities/EntityUtils/ai/Herd.java#L126-L147).
- Jam recovery: if more than `members/4` path attempts fail in a tick, the heading is reversed with small noise and `failedPathTicks` grows; the state ends early when `failedPathTicks >= members * 2` — [Herd.java L57, L196-L206](https://github.com/Interactive-Modding/Jurassic-Reborn/blob/4a9a21b6ad7cea718534abd31913cccca2a6d98e/src/main/java/mod/reborn/server/entity/ai/Herd.java#L196-L206).
- Fleeing: heading = average of angles away from all attackers; centroid pull is reduced x4 — [Herd.java L78-L95](https://github.com/Interactive-Modding/Jurassic-Reborn/blob/4a9a21b6ad7cea718534abd31913cccca2a6d98e/src/main/java/mod/reborn/server/entity/ai/Herd.java#L78-L95).
- Merge/split: members farther than sqrt(2048) ≈ 45 blocks from the centroid split into a new herd; herds within 16 blocks merge if under `maxHerdSize` — [Herd.java L103-L112, L237-L300](https://github.com/Interactive-Modding/Jurassic-Reborn/blob/4a9a21b6ad7cea718534abd31913cccca2a6d98e/src/main/java/mod/reborn/server/entity/ai/Herd.java#L237-L300).
- Untamed Wilds `HerdEntity`: leader plus list, max size, `radius = 8`; every 10 ticks members within the radius look along the leader's look vector (heading alignment); members beyond `splitOffDistance = 1024` (sq) leave; herds merge within 16 blocks — [HerdEntity](https://github.com/RayTrace082/untamedwilds/blob/4891218ae8d56d15f1689cee17e5aca9e96ca99a/src/main/java/untamedwilds/entity/HerdEntity.java). Followers wander to `DefaultRandomPos.getPos(leader, 7, 3)` (a random spot within 7 blocks of the leader), with no separation term — [SmartWanderGoal L59-L64](https://github.com/RayTrace082/untamedwilds/blob/4891218ae8d56d15f1689cee17e5aca9e96ca99a/src/main/java/untamedwilds/entity/ai/SmartWanderGoal.java#L59-L64).
- Untamed Wilds pack attack: `MeleeAttackCircleHerd` circles the target at radius 6 with a per-mob random phase offset (`cos(offset + tick/40) * 6`) — [MeleeAttackCircleHerd L121, L158-L159](https://github.com/RayTrace082/untamedwilds/blob/4891218ae8d56d15f1689cee17e5aca9e96ca99a/src/main/java/untamedwilds/entity/ai/MeleeAttackCircleHerd.java#L158-L159).
- Prehistoric Fauna: `FollowHerdLeaderGoal` is the vanilla schooling-fish pattern; every 10 ticks each follower calls `navigation.moveTo(leader, 1)` while within 11 blocks — [FollowHerdLeaderGoal](https://github.com/superlord9362/Prehistoric-Fauna/blob/e00d5241b0f28cbe8d2d55d2018b812b983680f6/src/main/java/superlord/prehistoricfauna/common/entity/goal/FollowHerdLeaderGoal.java), [HerdDinosaurEntity L77-L88](https://github.com/superlord9362/Prehistoric-Fauna/blob/e00d5241b0f28cbe8d2d55d2018b812b983680f6/src/main/java/superlord/prehistoricfauna/common/entity/HerdDinosaurEntity.java#L77-L88).
- Wyrmroost (current) has flock goals for flyers (`WRFlockFlyInCirclesGoal`, `WRFlockFlyAwayGoal`, `WRReturnToFlockGoal`) — [goal directory](https://github.com/Shannieann/Wyrmroost/tree/a1f9934b4f5207a6a35504a4a0ad7cc7c133cdbf/src/main/java/com/github/shannieann/wyrmroost/entity/dragon/ai/goals); not read in detail (air, not ground).
- Naturalist, Critters and Companions, Creatures and Beasts: no herd or flock classes found by file-name scan (trees in the table above).

### Inferences
- Pathing every follower straight to the leader (Prehistoric Fauna) is the textbook cause of the clustering Project Nublar sees: all paths share one goal and pile up behind the leader.
- Jurassic Reborn's design is the closest precedent for a 20-animal herd: a shared heading, per-member targets that include separation, and a herd-level failure counter that turns the whole herd around. Its weaknesses are (1) circular separation from centre points, which under-separates long animals walking nose-to-tail and over-separates them side by side; (2) targets snapped to `getHeight` surface, which ignores the body's footprint; (3) a repath gate tied to path end distance only.
- The candidate's snout-to-tail capsule spacing is strictly more expressive than every surveyed mod. The herd-level "reverse heading on mass path failure" rule from JurassiCraft is a cheap addition the candidate does not yet list.

### Gaps
- No mod found with formation slots, lane assignment, or capsule/OBB-based spacing; Discord-only or closed-source dinosaur mods (Prehistoric Nature, Born in Chaos) could not be checked.
- No issue-tracker reports specific to herd jams were found for Jurassic Reborn, Prehistoric Fauna or Untamed Wilds (searches for "stuck" returned unrelated issues).

## Q5. Is any mod clearly better than the candidate hybrid in some area?

### Takeaway
No mod beats the candidate on large-body movement or herd spacing. Four mods are better in narrow areas worth borrowing: Aquamirae (loader-neutral parts for the Fabric port), Iron's Spells (part offsets in model pixels scaled by entity scale), BOMD/MultipartEntities (oriented boxes for exact hits without extra entities), and Jurassic Reborn (herd-level jam recovery). TF is the best current reference for server-authoritative part sync on NeoForge 26.1.

### Cited Findings
- Cross-loader parts from common code: Aquamirae's four mixins on `Level`, `ServerLevel` and `ServerLevel$EntityCallbacks` — see Q1 links ([MixinLevel](https://github.com/ObscuriaLithium/aquamirae/blob/a468d77cc443a932d56a7aa34303f4e435eca3b6/common/src/main/java/dev/obscuria/aquamirae/integration/mixin/MixinLevel.java)).
- Growth-friendly part layout: offsets authored in model pixels, rotated with body yaw and scaled by `getScale()`; part size also scaled — [IceSpiderPartEntity L35-L39, L102](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/entity/mobs/ice_spider/IceSpiderPartEntity.java#L35-L39).
- Oriented (rotated) hit boxes in a hierarchy with pivots, no part entities, projectile raycast to name the part hit — [CompoundOrientedBox](https://github.com/barribob/MultipartEntities/blob/895e77a82ccc695cfd13d68c9638ce3b5f6bb458/src/main/java/io/github/stuff_stuffs/multipart_entities/common/util/CompoundOrientedBox.java), [GauntletHitboxes](https://github.com/barribob/bosses-of-mass-destruction/blob/2fbd0dc79bea498bcad755c4ad9969055dc452c7/src/main/kotlin/net/barribob/boss/mob/mobs/gauntlet/GauntletHitboxes.kt).
- Herd jam recovery and shared heading — [Jurassic Reborn Herd.java L196-L206](https://github.com/Interactive-Modding/Jurassic-Reborn/blob/4a9a21b6ad7cea718534abd31913cccca2a6d98e/src/main/java/mod/reborn/server/entity/ai/Herd.java#L196-L206).
- Server-authoritative part position+size sync on NeoForge 26.1.2 — [TFPart.writeData/readData](https://github.com/TeamTwilight/twilightforest/blob/28117eab67614dd31965c73b78b178f8ad7fb92d/src/main/java/twilightforest/entity/TFPart.java).
- Path smoothing by collinearity and line-of-sight pruning — [NotIdioticNavigation](https://github.com/iron431/irons-spells-n-spellbooks/blob/e4056af90302d37eb1739f5ff05020b020e6e252/src/main/java/io/redspace/ironsspellbooks/entity/mobs/wizards/fire_boss/NotIdioticNavigation.java).

### Inferences
- Section hit parts: the BOMD approach (one entity, `getBoundingBox()` returns an AABB subclass holding OBBs) avoids part entity ids, tracking and packets, and gives rotated boxes that fit a diagonal neck or tail better than axis-aligned parts. Risks: it overrides the entity's own bounding box, which vanilla uses for block collision, entity lookups and culling; it depends on mixins into `Box`/`AABB` and `VoxelShapes`; and BOMD itself turns it off for Better Combat. On balance it is a credible alternative for hit detection only, and should be weighed against the candidate's `PartEntity`-style parts in the oriented-shapes notes.
- MHLib's client-elected bone sync is the only bone-accurate server hitbox design found, but it trusts a client and lags by at least one round trip; for Project Nublar, where the server already owns growth and pose, a server-side spine is simpler and safer.
- Iron's Spells-style pruning (drop collinear nodes, skip to the end on clear sight) would help herd animals stop zig-zagging on open ground, but its traversal test is a centre line; for a wide body it must be replaced by a width or capsule sweep, which the candidate's corner-clearance check would supply.
- Nothing surveyed handles a body-length clearance check at corners, turn-in-place for navigation, or non-circular herd spacing. On these points the candidate goes beyond every mod checked.

### Gaps
- Closed-source boss mods (Born in Chaos, Bosses'Rise) and Discord-only discussions could not be checked; one of them could contain better movement logic.
- Whether `AABB` subclassing (the BOMD trick) still works on MC 26.x was not verified.
